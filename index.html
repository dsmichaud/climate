Station ClimateYear-round climate from a station’s own record

The climate of one airport weather station, built from its own observations.

Enter an ASOS or AWOS station ID, choose the years, and build a report on temperature, clouds, precipitation and snow, sun and moon, humidity, wind, heat index and wind chill, outdoor thermal stress, growing season and solar energy. Hover any chart to read its values; click it to open a table built for that chart, with every year of the record for the date, hour or month you picked.

Each station opens on a live page that puts the current observation and the NWS forecast in climate context. Behind it are climate pages for the year and each month, histories for every year, month and day (with raw METARs and 1-minute wind), NWS-style climate graphs and monthly tables, records and return periods, a storm catalog, a climate calendar, trends, data quality checks, an event finder, a conditions explorer, a wind resource screening, a construction work-window planner, and side-by-side comparison with up to three other stations. Press / anywhere to jump to a page or date.

Hourly reports

Routine METAR reports come from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive. Twenty years takes about a minute the first time; downloads are kept in this browser, so later reports open quickly.

Measured snow

Where the NWS publishes a daily climate report for the station, its snowfall and snow depth are added, with snow-to-liquid ratios by day and month.

No reanalysis

Precipitation, wind, clouds and humidity come from the station. Sun and moon are computed. Sunshine for solar energy and thermal stress is estimated from the station’s sky cover.
